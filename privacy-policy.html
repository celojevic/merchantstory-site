MerchantStory Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

MerchantStory is a single-player game with no ads and no analytics. This page explains what information the game uses, where it goes, and how to remove it.

Your save game

Your save game is always kept on your device. With Cloud save on (the default, in Settings), a copy is also stored with our cloud provider, Unity Gaming Services, so you can restore it on a new device or after reinstalling. The cloud copy contains your game progress (heroes, items, gold and so on) and, to help you choose between two saves, when it was saved and the model name of the device it was saved on (for example "Pixel 8"). It does not contain your name, email address or contacts.

To keep the cloud copy, Unity Gaming Services creates a game account for you, identified by a random player ID:

  • On Android, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, that account is linked to your Play Games profile, so your save follows your Google account to other devices. Unity receives your Play Games player ID for this; it does not receive your email address or password.
  • Otherwise the account is anonymous and belongs to this installation of the game.

When the game talks to Unity Gaming Services, Unity also receives technical information needed to run and secure the service: your IP address, a random installation ID, the device model and operating system version, the game's version, and diagnostic information about the connection. Unity processes this on our behalf, as described in Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. The information is sent over an encrypted connection and is not sold or used for advertising.

Turning cloud save off and deleting your data

You can turn Cloud save off at any time in the game's Settings → Cloud save; the game then stops sending your save. To delete your data, tap Delete cloud save and account in the same place: this deletes the cloud copy of your save and your game account from Unity Gaming Services, and turns cloud save off. The save on your device stays until you uninstall the game. If you can no longer open the game, email us (below) and we will delete your data.

Purchases

The full-game unlock is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your payment details. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The game only receives a record that the unlock was bought, which it uses to open the rest of the game and to restore your purchase.

Google Play Games

On Android, the game uses Google Play Games to show your achievements and, with Cloud save on, to sign in to your cloud save (see above). If you are signed in to Play Games, Google receives which achievements you have earned in this game, under your Play Games profile. This is handled by Google, as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Updates

On Android, the game asks Google Play whether a newer version is available and, if you agree, Google Play downloads and installs it. No information about you is sent for this beyond what Google Play already handles.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example, when a party comes home). These are created on the device and are not sent through any server. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in your phone's settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
