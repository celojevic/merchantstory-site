MerchantStory Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

MerchantStory is a single-player game. It has no accounts, no ads and no servers of its own. This page explains what information the game uses.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for, collect or share any personal or device information, and it has no analytics. Your save game is stored only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game.

Purchases

The full-game unlock is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your payment details. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The game only receives a record that the unlock was bought, which it uses to open the rest of the game and to restore your purchase.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example, when a party comes home). These are created on the device and are not sent through any server. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in your phone's settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
